Privacy Policy for Call Fence

Effective Date: August 26, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Ritesh Sethia ("we," "our," or "us") built Call Fence as a freemium app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Call Fence.

By using Call Fence, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Information We Do NOT Collect

Call Fence is designed to keep as little data as possible off your device:

  • We do not require or offer account creation or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not access your Contacts, Photos, Location, Microphone, or Camera — the app never requests these permissions.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

The Number Patterns You Add

The phone number patterns you create in Call Fence (e.g. "+91 98765 XXXXX") and their optional labels are stored only in a local, app-private storage container on your device, shared between the Call Fence app and its Call Directory Extension so both can read the same list. This data:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Is used solely to generate the list of blocked phone numbers that iOS's own Call Blocking & Identification system enforces.

If you delete Call Fence from your device, this data is deleted along with it.

Purchase Information (Subscriptions)

If you subscribe to Call Fence Pro, your purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, and subscription status is managed via RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. See Section 4 for details on what RevenueCat receives.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we collect no personal information directly, there is very little for us to use. The limited purchase-related data described in Section 4 is used only to:

  • Verify and unlock your Pro subscription entitlement.
  • Let you restore your purchase on a new device or after reinstalling.

We do NOT use any information to:

  • Sell your data to third parties.
  • Build advertising profiles.
  • Make automated decisions that affect you.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We have no server of our own to share it from — the only information that exists outside your device is the subscription/purchase data described below, held by Apple and RevenueCat.

4. Third-Party Services

Call Fence uses one third-party service:

ServicePurposePrivacy Policy
RevenueCatSubscription management — validates App Store purchases and unlocks Pro featuresRevenueCat Privacy Policy

RevenueCat receives an anonymous, app-generated identifier and your purchase/subscription history (product purchased, renewal status). It does not receive your blocked number patterns, labels, or any other content from within the app. RevenueCat does not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Apple's App Store also processes your payment directly; we never see or store your payment details.

5. Data Retention

  • Number patterns and labels: Stored on your device only, for as long as the app is installed. Deleted automatically when you uninstall the app, or manually when you delete a series in the app.
  • Purchase/subscription data: Retained by Apple and RevenueCat per their own retention policies, for as long as needed to manage your subscription and support restore-purchase requests.

6. Data Security

Your number patterns and labels are stored in an app-private container that only Call Fence and its Call Directory Extension can access — no other app on your device can read this data. All communication with RevenueCat's servers (for subscription status) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but Call Fence's design — keeping your actual data on-device and never transmitting it — minimizes what could ever be exposed.

7. Your Rights

Because Call Fence stores your blocked-number data only on your own device, you already have full control over it: you can view, edit, or delete any series directly in the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

For the limited purchase-related data held by RevenueCat, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the purchase data associated with your anonymous app identifier.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of that data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at riteshsethia7@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
