Getting started

How do I block a number series?

Tap +, enter a phone number pattern using X as a wildcard digit — for example +1 555 234-XXXX blocks all 10,000 numbers from 234-0000 through 234-9999. You can optionally add a label so you remember why you blocked it later.

Nothing is being blocked — why?

Call Fence needs Call Blocking & Identification turned on for it under Settings → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification. The app will show a banner and a direct button to get there if it detects this isn't enabled yet. If it's already on and a call still isn't blocked, double check the number actually falls within a pattern you've added — Call Fence only blocks the exact ranges you've defined.

Why does it take a moment after I add a series?

Call Fence hands your patterns to iOS's own call-blocking system, which needs a short window to generate and register every number in the range — that's what the progress bar reflects. Don't remove the app from the background while it's running, or the update may not finish; if that happens, Call Fence will tell you on next launch and ask you to add the series again.

Free vs. Pro

What's the difference?

Free includes up to 2 blocked series and 100,000 numbers total. Pro removes the series limit and raises the total to 1,200,000 numbers, billed monthly or yearly. Exact current limits are always shown in the app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open Call Fence, tap the gearshape icon (top-left) → Manage Subscriptions. This opens Apple's own subscription management screen, where you can change plans or cancel. You can also manage it directly under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

What happens to my series if I cancel?

You keep full Pro access until your current billing period actually ends — cancelling just stops the next renewal. If your stored series still exceed the Free plan's limits once your Pro period ends, Call Fence will show you exactly which series are about to be removed (the most recently added ones first) before removing anything, and lets you re-subscribe instead if you'd rather keep them.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases go through Apple, and only Apple can issue refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com — we don't have the ability to process refunds directly.

Privacy
