3. How Nekodoro Works

Nekodoro is a focus timer built around a virtual cat companion. You start a focus session; if you complete it, you earn coins. If you abandon a focus session early, your cat loses one of its 9 lives; if it runs out of lives, your cat "dies" (a reversible, in-app state, not a real animal) until revived. Progress, coins, and your cat are cosmetic, in-app elements with no cash value outside the App.

Optional features vary by platform:

  • Android: Focus Mode can block distracting apps during a session, using Android's Usage Access and overlay permissions. This depends on Android system behavior and device manufacturer restrictions; we cannot guarantee every app switch is caught instantly.
  • iOS: No equivalent app-blocking capability exists on iOS, so Focus Mode is not offered there.
