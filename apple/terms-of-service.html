3. How Call Blocking Actually Works — Important Limitations

Call Fence does not intercept or screen your calls itself. Instead, it hands the phone number patterns you define to Apple's own CallKit Call Directory system, and iOS itself — not Call Fence — decides which calls to block, based on whether Call Blocking & Identification is enabled in your Settings and whatever entries iOS has actually accepted and stored.

You understand and agree that:

  • Blocking is not guaranteed. iOS imposes its own undocumented limits on how many phone numbers a Call Directory extension can register, independent of your subscription tier. Call Fence enforces conservative caps intended to stay safely under those limits, but we cannot guarantee that iOS will accept, retain, or correctly enforce every entry at all times.
  • A pattern must finish syncing to take effect. If the app or your device is closed or loses power while a series is being added, that series may not have synced to iOS and will be automatically removed the next time you open the app, with a notice asking you to add it again.
  • We cannot verify individual blocked calls. Neither the App nor its Call Directory Extension is invoked at call time — iOS enforces blocking directly — so we have no way to confirm after the fact whether any specific call was blocked.
  • Emergency and other critical calls are governed entirely by iOS. Call Fence has no ability to override iOS's own call-handling behavior.

Call Fence is provided as a tool to reduce unwanted calls, not as a guarantee that any specific call will never reach you.
